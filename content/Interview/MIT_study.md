Based on current hiring trends for **Machine Learning Engineer** and **AI Engineer** roles, companies are looking for engineers who can **build, train, deploy, and maintain production AI systems**, not just train models in notebooks. Modern interviews heavily emphasize **Python, ML fundamentals, Deep Learning, LLMs, MLOps, system design, and practical projects**. ([Interview Query][1])

Since I've seen your background (PyTorch, OCR, NLP, RAG, TTS, Document AI), I would organize your preparation like an **interview syllabus** rather than an academic syllabus.
