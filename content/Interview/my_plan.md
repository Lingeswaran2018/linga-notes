# Our Study Plan

We'll go topic by topic and go deep.

1. **Python & Coding**
2. **Machine Learning**
3. **Deep Learning**
4. **Transformers**
5. **LLMs**
6. **RAG**
7. **OCR**
8. **Speech AI**
9. **Knowledge Graphs**
10. **Projects from your CV**
11. **System Design**
12. **Mock Interview**

For each topic, I'll:

* Explain the concepts from beginner to advanced.
* Ask interview-style questions (easy → medium → hard).
* Explain the expected answers.
* Cover common follow-up questions.
* Include coding questions where relevant.
* Connect the discussion back to the projects and experience listed on your CV so you're prepared to defend every line. 

This approach will prepare you not only to answer questions, but also to confidently discuss the experience you've highlighted on your resume.
