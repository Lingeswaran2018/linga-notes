# 1. Deep Learning in Supervised Learning

## Overview

Supervised learning is the most widely used deep learning paradigm. Models learn from **labeled datasets**, where every input has a corresponding target output. The objective is to learn a mapping from inputs to outputs for prediction on unseen data. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## Common Neural Networks

- Feedforward Neural Networks (FNN)
- Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLP)
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN)
- Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN)
- Transformers

## Applications

- Image Classification
- Spam Detection
- Credit Risk Prediction
- Disease Diagnosis
- Customer Churn Prediction

# 11. Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN)

## Overview

GANs consist of two competing neural networks:

- Generator
- Discriminator

The Generator creates synthetic data, while the Discriminator distinguishes between real and generated samples.

## Workflow

Random Noise

↓

Generator

↓

Fake Image

↓

Discriminator

↓

Real / Fake

## Applications

- AI Art
- Super Resolution
- Deepfake Detection
- Data Augmentation

# 12. GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer)

## Overview

GPT is a family of Transformer-based autoregressive language models that are first **pre-trained on large text corpora** and then adapted for downstream tasks. They generate text by predicting the next token in a sequence. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

## Characteristics

- Transformer Decoder Architecture
- Self-Attention Mechanism
- Large-Scale Pretraining
- Few-Shot and Zero-Shot Learning

## Applications

- Chatbots
- Code Generation
- Translation
- Summarization
- Question Answering
- Content Generation

## Examples

- ChatGPT
- GPT-4
- GPT-5 family
- Microsoft Copilot

# Summary

- **Supervised Learning** is the foundation of most predictive deep learning applications.
- **Computer Vision**, **Computer Audition**, **NLP**, and **Sequence Learning** are major application domains of supervised deep learning.
- **Unsupervised Learning** enables representation learning and feature extraction from unlabeled data.
- **Reinforcement Learning** teaches agents through interaction and rewards, while **Deep Reinforcement Learning** scales RL using neural networks.
- **Deep Q Learning** is one of the earliest and most influential deep RL algorithms.
- **Generative Learning** creates new content, with **GANs** specializing in image generation and **GPT** excelling in text generation.
- **Graph Neural Networks** extend deep learning to relational and graph-structured data, enabling applications in recommendation systems, molecular science, and social networks.
